Lab Updates

FALL 2026

LingoBrain Lab launches at the University of Alabama

Dr. Yanyu Xiong opens the LingoBrain Lab in the Department of Speech, Language and Hearing Sciences, continuing her research on the neural basis of language in bilinguals, clinical populations, and late-talking children.

JAN 2026

Dr. Xiong speaks at the CYDI Senior Affiliate Speaker Series

Dr. Xiong presented "Neural Plasticity Across the Life Span Through the Lens of Neurobiology of Language" as part of the Center for Youth Development and Intervention's Senior Affiliate Speaker Series of the Department of Psychology at the University of Alabama, held January 27, 2026.
