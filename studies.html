Below are studies currently recruiting participants in the LingoBrain Lab. If you're interested or have questions, reach out using the contact information listed for each study.

Bilingualism Actively Recruiting

Neurocognitive Correlates of Bilingual Sentence Processing

Goals
  • Neuroplasticity of second-language (L2) grammatical learning (Mandarin) from adolescence to adulthood, using fMRI & EEG
Eligibility
  • L2 learners: Native English speakers; ≤3 months formal Chinese training; right-handed
  • Native speakers: Native Mandarin speakers; ≤3 years in the U.S.; right-handed
Methods
  • Visit 1: survey + standardized behavioral tests (60–100 min)
  • Visit 2: MRI session, two language tasks (60 min)
  • Visit 3: EEG session, same tasks (60 min)
Compensation
  • $10/hour for Visit 1, $20/hour for Visit 2 and Visit 3

Child Development Actively Recruiting

Preschooler Language Development

Goals
  • Identify social, demographic, environmental & neurocognitive factors related to preschoolers' language development
  • Explore potential markers for children with delayed language development
Eligibility
  • Child ages 1.5–5 years
Methods
  • Families choose a video-recording session, an EEG session, or both
  • Video-recording (~2 hrs): Parent questionnaires; interactive language tasks with the child, recorded on video
  • EEG session (~1 hr): Child computer-based language tasks; parent questionnaires
Compensation
  • Child: $10 per session ($20 for both); Parent/guardian: $20 per session ($40 for both)
  • +$10 travel reimbursement if living more than 20 miles away

Language Disorders Actively Recruiting

Neurocognitive Mechanisms of Post-Stroke Language Impairment

Goals
  • Relate language/cognitive assessment data to structural and functional connectivity of brain & lesion parameters
Eligibility
  • Self-reported stroke diagnosis
  • Stroke to a cerebral hemisphere or subcortical region
  • Native English speakers, right-handed
  • No pre-stroke intellectual disability or neurological disorder
  • Early chronic (12–24 weeks) or chronic (24+ weeks) stage
Methods
  • Visit 1: cognitive/language testing, ~1 hour
  • Visit 2: MRI session, ~1 hour
Compensation
  • $10/hour for Visit 1; $20/hour for Visit 2
